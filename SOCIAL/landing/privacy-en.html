Using the app without an account

You can use Bucktrail without creating an account. In that case accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, goals and recurring items stay only on your device: we don’t receive them and can’t see them. The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

Device features work locally too:

  • Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint: the app lock uses the operating system’s recognition. The app only gets the result (unlocked or not), never your biometric data.
  • Notifications: reminders and budget or balance alerts are local notifications created by the device.
  • Export: PDF, CSV and JSON files are created only when you ask, and you choose who to share them with and where.
  • Widgets and shortcuts: they read and write data on the device.
  • Demo mode: uses sample data and syncs nothing.

Account and sync

If you create the optional account to sync several devices, we process:

  • your account email address and password (the password is handled by the sign-in service and is not stored in plain text);
  • the data you sync: accounts, transactions (with name, amount, date, category and notes), budgets, savings goals and recurring items;
  • technical sync data: record identifiers, sync times and the IP address you connect from.

We use this data only to sign you in and keep your devices in step. The legal basis is providing the service you asked for by creating the account (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We don’t use it for advertising, profiling or statistics, and we don’t look at it unless you ask us to, to fix a problem.

Data travels over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and the provider encrypts it at rest on the server; each account can read only its own data. On the device, the database sits in the app’s protected storage. [TO BE COMPLETED: who sends the account service emails (sign-up confirmation, password reset): Supabase or another email service].

If you log out, the app tries to send your latest changes and then deletes the data from the device. Synced data stays on the server until you delete the account.

App sync and Advanced Protection

If you use the optional account to sync your devices and turn on Advanced Protection (Settings → Security), the server stores your data only in encrypted form (AES-256-GCM), together with the wrapped keys, which can’t be used without your security phrase or recovery key, and the technical encryption parameters. Still visible are the account, the identifiers, the number and type of records, the approximate size of the data, the sync times and the IP address you sync from. Nobody, not even the service operator, can decrypt the data.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from the app: Settings → Delete account, with a double confirmation. Deletion removes the account from the server and, in the same operation, all synced data: accounts, transactions, budgets, goals, recurring items and any encrypted Advanced Protection data. The app then deletes the data from the device. It can’t be undone: if you want to keep a copy, export your data first. You can also ask for deletion by writing to ciao@bucktrailapp.com from the account’s email address.

Waitlist and website

If you sign up we record your email address, your consent and, if present in the link you arrived from, the campaign parameters (UTM) to measure where sign-ups come from. To complete the sign-up we send you a confirmation email: until you click the link, your address is not added to the list. We use it only to let you know when Bucktrail is available. The legal basis is consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Signing up is optional. Without an email and consent we can’t send you the notice.

If you email us, we use your address and the content of the message only to reply. The landing page uses no cookies. To know how many people visit it and which pages they come from we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits in aggregate, without cookies and without storing anything on your device, and doesn’t identify you or follow you on other sites. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in knowing whether the site works (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); you can object by writing to us. The links to Instagram, TikTok and YouTube lead to third-party sites, which have their own policies.

Withdrawal and your rights

You can withdraw your waitlist consent at any time through the unsubscribe link in the email or by writing to ciao@bucktrailapp.com. Withdrawal doesn’t affect the lawfulness of earlier processing. You can ask for access, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interest, under the conditions set by the GDPR. In the app you can exercise many of these rights yourself: edit your data, export it as CSV or JSON, delete the account. We reply to requests within one month. You can lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali) or with the authority in your country.
