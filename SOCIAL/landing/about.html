Who is behind Bucktrail

I’m Matteo Cocivera and I created Bucktrail. I work alone, with no investors: it’s an independent project and I make every decision.

Why I built it

Bucktrail started from a personal need. I’ve always needed to keep my income and spending under control, and to know at any moment how much I’m spending, where, and what’s left. For years I did it on paper and in Excel, but I never had anything automated: fixed expenses, the balance after fixed expenses, recurring items.

The apps I tried weren’t for me. Even paid ones had ads that made me uninstall them, and they were colourful, confusing and not serious. I wanted the simplicity of writing with pen and paper: an essential style.

What I believe

  • No ads. A firm choice that won’t change.
  • No subscription. I don’t believe in subscriptions. I think paying once, about 5 euros, is a conscious choice, and the first step to understanding how you handle your money.
  • Everything in one app. Fixed expenses, recurring items, budgets, goals and statistics, with no need to go back to the spreadsheet.
